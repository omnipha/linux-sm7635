## Bounded RAM-backed postmarketOS PID-1 handoff (#145, successor host-only, 2026-10-28)

Run 49/#139 proved a staged child/chroot and key-only non-PTY USB SSH on the stock 6.1.84 kernel, then ended with five stock slot-A hashes and stock Android verified. Its PID 1 remained diagnostic BusyBox. The successor changes only the run-43 generic ramdisk's `/init`: after the old diagnostic `:22`, USB gadget, modules, WLAN and 150 s recovery setup, it leaves the diagnostic console shell as a child and makes the *actual PID 1* wait for a mode-0600 current-boot-ID request and a root-owned, mode-0700 SHA-256-pinned transition script. Invalid requests are removed without replacing PID 1. PID 1 records a monotonic timestamp upon reaching the console tail **without requiring `/run/keep` yet**. A later valid request requires the hold and waits at least 151.01 monotonic seconds from that timestamp with the hold present throughout the wait, so the previously armed 150 s recovery child has passed its check while the diagnostic service is still available. If the hold or monotonic clock is missing when the request arrives, the request is rejected; invalid uptime at the console tail permanently disables the handoff. No guard or PID-1 cutover was measured with this successor.
